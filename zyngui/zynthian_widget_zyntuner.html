zyntuner

Low CPU guitar tuner. Stereo audio passes through unchanged.

Widget

Play one string at a time. The note name is shown with its deviation in cents. The needle is left of centre when the note is flat and right when it is sharp. The colour is orange within 15 cents and green when in tune (within 3 cents). "--" means that no pitch is detected.

Controls

  • A4 reference (415 .. 466 Hz): tuning reference, normally 440 Hz
  • Threshold (-80 .. -20 dB): signals below this level are ignored
  • Bypass: stops the detection. Audio still passes

Tips

The left and right inputs are mixed for the detection. Raise the threshold if noise triggers the tuner.
